# Village Beta Balance Audit

Date: July 7, 2026

## Verdict

Village choice is safe for first beta as an identity and social preference, not a hard mechanical balance lane. Do not add village buffs, village nerfs, or catch-up mechanics until real population and activity data exists.

## Current Villages

- Stormveil Village
- Ashen Leaf Village
- Frostfang Village
- Moonshadow Village

Existing launch copy already tells players village choice has no hidden stat bonuses. Keep that message prominent.

## Balance Questions For Beta

| Question | Metric |
| --- | --- |
| Are villages evenly chosen? | Character creation count by village. |
| Does one village retain better? | Day 1/7 retention by village. |
| Does one village dominate PvP? | PvP win rate by village and level band. |
| Does one village dominate war/village systems? | Only measure if war is staffed/enabled. |
| Does one village confuse players? | Feedback tags and first-session exit by village. |

## Safe First Beta State

- Village is flavor, social identity, story route, and home context.
- No hidden stat bonuses.
- No first-week village power tuning.
- Village/Sector War systems are monitored or gated.
- Kage, Elder, ANBU, war, and treasury systems should not be marketed as stable first-week competitive seasons.

## Risks

| Risk | Why it matters | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Population imbalance | One village may become the default from art/name preference | Do not punish popularity; use content/copy first. |
| Low-pop village disadvantage | War/social systems can snowball | Keep war seasons staffed or delayed. |
| Hidden-power assumptions | Players may think village choice secretly affects stats | Keep explicit no-hidden-bonus copy. |
| Leadership disputes | Kage/Elder/ANBU systems are high-drama by design | Staff before broad enabling. |
| War economy inflation | War rewards are large | Receipt review and admin coverage required. |

## Beta Patch 1 Recommendation

No village balance number changes. Recommended work:

1. Track village choice at character creation.
2. Track first-session completion by village.
3. Keep village flavor strong but mechanically transparent.
4. Delay competitive village season claims until population is large enough.
5. If one village is underpicked, improve presentation/content rather than adding power.
