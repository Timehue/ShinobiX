# Beta Patch 1 Notes Draft

Date: July 7, 2026

## Title

Beta Patch 1: First-Week Clarity, Monitoring, and Safer Rewards

## Player-Facing Draft

Thanks for helping test ShinobiX. This first beta patch focuses on making the early game clearer, keeping rewards trustworthy, and making it easier for the team to respond to reports quickly.

### What We Are Watching

- New-player Academy completion.
- First mission claim and reward delivery.
- PvP match settlement and battle receipts.
- Mission, hunt, bank, hospital, and training loops.
- Mobile layout issues in core gameplay.
- Early ryo pacing and hospital recovery feedback.

### Beta Systems

Some systems are intentionally monitored or gated during beta:

- PvP and Ranked PvP are available with monitoring.
- Battle Towers, Endless Tower, Pet Arena, and Pet Ladder are beta systems.
- Weekly Boss rewards remain gated until contribution settlement is fully server-authoritative.
- Village War and Sector War are staffed/soft-launch systems.
- Hollow Gate is desktop-first beta content for now.
- Player AI image generation is not broadly enabled.

### Balance

No broad combat, XP, or economy rebalance is included in this patch. We are collecting real data first. Please report stuck fights, missing rewards, confusing reward messages, and any economy issues.

### How To Report

When reporting an issue, include your player name, screen, what you clicked, platform, approximate time, and a screenshot or battle id if it involves combat or rewards.

## Internal Notes

- Do not publish claims that level 50-100 or PvP are fully balanced.
- Keep high-risk release flags unset unless staff explicitly approves.
- Patch notes should be honest about monitored systems.
- If a reward bug is fixed, mention the affected source and whether old claims were corrected.
