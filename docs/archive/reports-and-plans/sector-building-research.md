# Sector buildings: MMORPG research and top ten adaptations

Design principles reviewed during the session audit on 2026-10-23. Building
identities and gameplay rules remain in [the building guide](../../sector-building-system.md).

## Ranked ideas for this world

Ranking favors a clear physical purpose, repeat visits and continuity with the
existing shinobi setting. The adaptations and ranking are our design judgments;
the principles do not establish this game's lore, payouts or exact mechanics.
All ten are represented in the current implementation.

| Priority | On-theme interaction | Identified premises and player purpose | Research principle adapted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First | Communal shelter restoration | Gorgewatch Post, Esplanade Repair Yard, Obsidian Road Station and other repair frontages: reserve gathered materials, perform repairs, see shared restoration and maintain it afterward. | Construction contracts plus shared work that changes the surrounding world. |
| Second | Tea counters that recognize regulars | Eda's Festival Tea Counter, Ren's Heartwood Tea Table and the existing tea houses: pay ryo, rest, meet the host and return during the established routine. | Inhabited outposts and NPC lives; tea service uses this game's existing recovery rules. |
| Third | Sealed courier relays | Village gatehouses and the harbor–deepwood counters: carry a sealed pouch between two real staffed destinations, including Tetsu's established circuit. | Delivery of useful supplies; routes and seals belong to this world. |
| Fourth | Evidence-led ruin investigations | Gorgewatch Post, Esplanade Record House, Obsidian Road Station, Greycliff Relay House and Greycliff Witness Enclosure: inspect three physical clues and preserve a supported Chronicle account. | Local stories discovered through exploration and NPCs. |
| Fifth | Guarded gate reception | All ordinary strongholds, Obsidian Stronghold and four village gates: speak with a named officer even when interior admission is unavailable. | Inhabited settlements; existing native admission rules retain authority. |
| Sixth | Watch posts with readable reports | South Watch Post, Gorgewatch Post and other watch counters: inspect approaches, record actual weather and file a report for later travelers. | Events grounded in local conditions and shared consequences. |
| Seventh | Regional workshop services | Stronghold workshops: select exact owned material grades and use existing regional recipes as local trust grows. | Production contracts as a reason to visit working premises; recipes remain native to this game. |
| Eighth | Entrusted contact windows | Shuttered Boathouse and Old Toll Booth: observe the contact's protocol, retain the sealed message and choose the correct speaking window. | NPC-led discoveries; the handling protocol is a shinobi-world adaptation. |
| Ninth | Shrine witness registers | Six existing shrines: read each local account and sign only deeds supported by completed work. | Location-specific storytelling; Chronicle records follow this game's established rules. |
| Tenth | Festival preparation and rehearsal | S52's Sunscar Welcome House, Sunscar Lantern House, Sunscar Performers' House and Eda's Festival Tea Counter: practice arrival duty, lantern preparation and the performance sequence, then take tea. | Varied local activities around inhabitants; native Sunscar admission remains separate. |

The complete inventory in the building guide distinguishes formerly unnamed
painted frontages from already named story-only sites. These are public counter
and work interactions; this feature does not add traversable room interiors to
every building.
