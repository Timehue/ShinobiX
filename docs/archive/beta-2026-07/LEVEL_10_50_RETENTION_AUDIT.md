# Level 10-50 Retention Audit

Date: July 7, 2026

## Verdict

The level 10-50 band has enough goals for beta, but it is where players can become overwhelmed. The main retention risk is not a lack of systems; it is unclear prioritization among missions, training, exams, professions, PvP, pets, clans, towers, and village features.

## Current Milestones

| Level band | Current beats | Retention risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10-14 | Path to Genin, Logbook chapters, training/jutsu growth | Player may not understand why level 15 matters. |
| 13 | Profession picker appears when no profession is set | Choice anxiety if benefits are not clear. |
| 15 | Genin rank, sub-Genin PvP protection lifts | PvP pressure starts; message should be clear. |
| 20 | Genin Exam level hold until exam is passed | Good retention gate if explained, frustrating if hidden. |
| 30 | Chunin rank and C-tier mission band | Good midgame identity beat. |
| 39 | Chunin Exam level hold until exam is passed | Must be clear before players hit the cap. |
| 50 | Jonin rank, A-tier mission band, story rank trials | Strong beta milestone, but late systems begin to crowd UI. |

## Existing Strengths

- Rank bands and exam gates are tested and mirrored server/client.
- Logbook objectives point players toward Genin and exams.
- Professions provide daily missions after level 13.
- Mission tiers at levels 15, 30, and 50 create visible payout upgrades.
- Combat stat growth is capped daily; training remains the main stat faucet.
- Competitive PvP protects sub-Genin players and uses server sessions/receipts.

## Friction Risks

| Risk | Details | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Too many menus after level 13 | Professions, pets, clan, town hall, marketplace, PvP, tower, and village systems compete | Keep Daily Briefing recommendations conservative and goal-oriented. |
| Profession regret | Healer, Vanguard, and Pet Tamer play very differently | Track profession choice and early abandonment; avoid buffs before data. |
| Exam cap surprise | Level holds can feel like a bug | Ensure Logbook and level-up copy mention the next exam before cap. |
| PvP reward farming | Base PvP win is 100 XP/75 ryo, with anti-farm systems | Watch repeat-win and alt reports through receipts. |
| Midgame economy noise | Bank, hospital, shop, jutsu, missions, and war systems all touch ryo | Do not tune ryo from anecdotes; use wallet histograms. |

## Beta Patch 1 Scope

Safe:

- Improve Logbook and Daily Briefing language around level 13 professions, level 15 Genin, level 20 Genin Exam, and level 39 Chunin Exam.
- Add admin-facing metrics for level distribution, profession choice, and exam pass rates.
- Add empty-state copy for low-population PvP/profession tasks.

Not safe without data:

- Changing XP curve.
- Changing profession XP thresholds.
- Changing PvP base rewards.
- Changing training rates.
- Buffing one village or profession because early players cluster elsewhere.

## Recommended Success Metrics

- 60% or more of players who reach level 10 open Logbook again.
- 50% or more of players who reach level 13 choose a profession within one session.
- Fewer than 10% of level-capped players report the Genin/Chunin hold as a bug.
- PvP settled/created ratio remains high, with no repeated unresolved battle ids.
