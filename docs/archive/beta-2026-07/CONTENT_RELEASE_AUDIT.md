# Content Release Audit

Date: July 7, 2026

## Verdict

ShinobiX has enough content for a public beta if the first cohort is told this is a beta and risky systems are gated or monitored. The core loop is strongest from level 1-25. Late-game exists, but should not be marketed as fully polished on mobile.

## Level Band Checklist

| Band | Content | Launch State |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1-10 | Academy path, starter pet, training, jutsu, inventory, spar, missions, Logbook, map intro | Ready |
| 10-25 | First Steps/Field Training goals, missions, hunts, Genin path, profession unlock | Ready with monitoring |
| 25-50 | C/B missions, Chunin path, clan join/found, early social systems, arena/PvP | Enable with warning |
| 50-75 | A/S hunts, Jonin path, village/clan war, towers, advanced crafting | Gate high-risk systems; monitor |
| 75-100 | Special Jonin, Kage/Elder goals, Hollow Gate, legacy/war goals | Desktop-first late beta |

## Repeatable Goals

- Daily missions and hunts.
- Stat and jutsu training.
- PvP/ranked goals.
- Pet training and pet arena.
- Towers and spire progression.
- Clan contribution and territory systems.
- Village upgrades and war rewards.
- Long-term Logbook/exam goals.

## Empty Or Underexplained Areas

| Area | Issue | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rare currencies | Many currencies appear before players understand their use. | Keep hints and add hover/tooltips after beta feedback. |
| War systems | Dense and high impact. | Gate/soft-launch with staff. |
| Hollow Gate | Late-game and complex. | Desktop-first label until mobile verified. |
| Creator tools | Powerful identity system, but moderation-sensitive. | Keep AI generation admin-only and monitor player-created bloodlines. |

## Launch Cuts/Gates

- Gate Weekly Boss rewards.
- Gate broad Village/Sector War promotion.
- Keep AI image generation admin-only.
- Treat admin and creator tools as desktop-first.
- Do not promote Hollow Gate as mobile-ready until verified.
