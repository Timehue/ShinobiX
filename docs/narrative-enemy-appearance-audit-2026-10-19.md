## Compatibility and limits

- HP curves, level gates, rewards and the catalog techniques' costs, effects and cooldowns are preserved. Selecting different schools, elements and loadouts changes combat matchups; this is more than a cosmetic rename. Veteran named encounters keep their larger rotation capacity. The existing four final-Kage kits are preserved.
- At this appearance-only audit, the paired trials still shared health and a turn. This limitation was subsequently removed: [independent twin trials](story-twin-trials-2026-10-19.md) now give both opponents separate health, turns, abilities and individual sprites.
- Anonymous troops can share role art. Named characters without a full-body asset use their exact portrait on the board. The audit does not claim every opponent now has a bespoke full-body animation.
- Existing sealed fights keep their stored combat fields. New fights receive the corrected identity and kit. Separately published administrator content and intentional image overrides were not fetched from production.
- Only the encounter's own ID or the server's private narrative-identity marker selects a themed kit. Reusing a rift boss image on a custom fighter cannot replace that fighter's authored abilities.
