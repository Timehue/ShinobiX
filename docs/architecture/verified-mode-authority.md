## Authority rules used for the audit

- PvP owns casual shinobi PvP, direct challenges, ranked shinobi PvP, and human-versus-human Sector War shinobi combat.
- Solo PvE owns normal shinobi-versus-AI combat: generic AI, World AI, ordinary AI raids, missions, story bosses, Academy sparring, Dungeon Warden, Weekly Boss, Endless, and Hollow Gate shinobi combat.
- Tower owns Battle Towers, Tower parties, Endless Spire, Clan Boss, and Tower PvP. The declared village-war mercenary battle is a valid headless Tower use; the wrong-owner Sector War garrison fallback is retired and the intended PvP surface remains absent.
- Pet Showdown/Coliseum is the turn-based pet owner and the only new paid Coliseum admission. Pet Warfront/Tactical is the positional pet family. Warfront and Tactical remain explicit game-mode surfaces even when they reuse that positional engine family; neither may be merged with or silently substituted for Showdown.
- Pet Gauntlet is a separate deterministic grid auto-battler and run-state machine, not an alias for Showdown or Warfront.
- New user-picked Pet Arena AI admission is retired; only exact issued-token
  recovery and capped settlement remain on its cinematic HTTP path. The
  rewarding Dungeon Rare Beast and Hollow Gate child seals also use exact
  parent-bound cinematic receipts. Ordinary live Pet Arena PvP uses a separate
  memory-only Socket.IO lifecycle over the same cinematic replay family and
  writes no rewards. The legacy pet duel and client-local presentation remain
  separately named compatibility boundaries; neither is a generic Pet owner or
  a substitute for Showdown, Warfront, Gauntlet, or cinematic authority.
- Chronicle/Card Clash is a separate combat family.
- Sector War dispatches to the correct combat owner and owns only shared territory admission and settlement.
- A rewarding authored encounter must have a server-owned encounter, enemy, outcome, proof, and idempotent settlement. A client outcome, client-selected enemy, or presentation replay is not proof.

Match status meanings:

- **MATCH** — mounted call path agrees with the owner boundary and server owns the result.
- **MATCH / LIMITED RECORD** — owner is correct, but only an expiring session/run record exists; there is no durable player-facing battle replay endpoint.
- **HEADLESS** — the server resolves a declared orchestration battle from sealed snapshots without an active fight client; it is not an interactive-mode substitute.
- **STAGED MISMATCH** — a replacement owner exists in code but the mounted public route still uses another runtime.
- **OWNER DECISION** — the mounted route has one current server-authoritative owner, but a future replatform choice remains explicitly unselected and may not be inferred from dormant or compatibility code.
- **SURFACE GAP** — a named product mode is admitted or required, but its independently mounted lifecycle is absent. Sharing its intended engine family does not erase the missing surface.
- **DEFECT** — the mounted route violates an explicit owner or server-authority rule.

### Current-registry reconciliation

The detailed tables below remain evidence from the named Phase-0 revision. The
[generated runtime-mode registry](../generated/runtime-mode-registry.md) has
precedence for current row status and adds distinctions that this snapshot did
not originally enumerate:

- new user-picked Pet Arena AI 1v1/2v2 admission is retired fail-closed. One
  separately registered compatibility row may recover the exact active
  pre-cutover unmarked token and server-replay its capped settlement; it has no
  `start` role and is not another paid Coliseum entry;
- the only new paid Pet Coliseum admission and progression settlement uses
  `pet-showdown`;
- ordinary Pet Arena live PvP 1v1/2v2 uses the server-sealed, memory-only
  `petduel:*` transport and `pet-cinematic-duel`, with no reward or rating write;
  the client supplies an explicitly selected or Auto-picked reserve and the
  server enforces exact one-pet/two-pet cardinality for 1v1/2v2;
- the broken public Pet Ranked queue is retired fail-closed and cannot pair or
  launch that no-reward realtime duel, while the older ranked compatibility
  route and a staged Showdown implementation remain separate and unconsumed;
  new legacy `rankedPet` notices are also retired while retained notices and
  proofs remain recoverable;
  the retained legacy route is itself defective because cinematic client
  playback can disagree with the legacy server replay;
- Hollow Gate pet is mounted on one server-sealed `pet-cinematic-duel` proof
  preselected by its parent binding. Duplicate starts reuse its exact seal and
  seed, and parent settlement requires the matching versioned engine/proof
  receipt. New Showdown admission and unbound legacy Showdown adoption fail
  closed; only the future replatform decision remains open; and
- Dungeon pet is now `pet-cinematic-duel`: the server validates the exact active
  run, selects the Rare Beast, replays the terminal input log, and stamps a Pet
  proof that the parent Dungeon settlement requires. Client-local presentation
  remains separately named but is not accepted as reward proof.
- Clan War shinobi 2v2 no longer admits new send/join/accept progression;
  retained queue records remain cleanup-only while the four-player PvP surface
  gap is unresolved.

Those rows must never be collapsed into a single `pet` authority. Showdown,
Warfront, Gauntlet, cinematic, legacy, and client-local engines remain separate
even when one client shell presents several of them.

## Reconciled outcomes and remaining gaps

1. **Sector garrison wrong-owner fallback retired.** New garrison combat returns
   `410`; the intended PvP-backed headless lifecycle remains an explicit surface
   gap rather than silently dispatching to Tower.
2. **Dungeon proof-chain gaps closed.** The Card and Pet child runtimes now stamp
   exact active-run terminal evidence, and final Dungeon settlement requires
   Warden, Card, and Pet wins before its redeemed-run receipt and reward commit.
3. **Live Pet Ranked retired fail-closed.** The public queue no longer pairs or
   launches the ordinary no-reward realtime duel. Re-admission requires one
   server-owned match proof shared by combat and rating; retained legacy
   compatibility remains separate.
4. **Paid Pet Coliseum has one new-admission owner.** Showdown owns its capped
   progression. User-picked cinematic AI admission is retired; the separately
   registered issued-token path is recovery/settlement only.
5. **Hollow Gate pet current authority is single-flight.** The parent preselects
   one cinematic proof and accepts only its exact versioned result. New
   Showdown admission and outcome-selectable legacy adoption fail closed; only
   a future replatform decision remains with the owner.
6. **Standalone Tactical Arena remains a surface gap.** Co-op Tactical is a
   separate zero-reward preview and does not supply the missing standalone
   lifecycle.
