# World Era Chapters

Status: all five sequential personal campaigns are implemented in the working tree following the October 1, 2026 era audit and the owner's instruction to close the IV/V gap. The harder campaigns supersede all five initial short chapters. Production deployment and live population tuning are separate from this implementation.

Design follow-up: IV is now a Master campaign and V a Grandmaster campaign, using the actual endgame Spire examinations and the receipt system established by I–III. The [original research](audits/eras-2026-10-01/endgame-research.md) remains a historical proposal; new normalized solo trials, commission variants and emblem art from that larger proposal remain future content. The [implementation record](audits/eras-2026-10-01/master-campaign-implementation.md) explains the rebased workloads, four-player requirement and controlled evidence.

Follow-up verification: [Campaign integration review](audits/eras-2026-10-01/integration-review.md) traces the I–III source settlements, progression dependencies and Hall flow, records corrections and distinguishes working integration from unresolved difficulty and engagement gaps.

## Player experience

Hall of Legends → World Eras connects each era to a permanent personal investigation. All five show every required campaign stage before acceptance. Players choose a narrative perspective, work through mandatory stages, pass combat examinations, and return their account to earn a cosmetic title. Both perspectives require the entire campaign and give different final accounts. Every stage has a briefing and a recorded conclusion. Later arrivals can participate and earn the same reward.

| Era | Personal chapter | Mandatory campaign or current chapter | Earned title |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | The Names on the First Roster | 30 C+ combat missions → 30 B+ combat missions → Story Tower floor 5 examination | Keeper of the First Roster |
| II | Corrections to the Deep Map | 75 B+ combat missions and 5 full Gates → 75 A+ combat missions and 5 more full Gates → Story Tower floor 7 examination | Bearer of the Return Map |
| III | The Border Has Two Sides | 150 A+ combat missions and 10 full Gates → 150 S combat missions and 10 more full Gates → Story Tower floor 9 examination → Story Tower floor 10 examination | Witness of the Border |
| IV | Four Seals on One Order | 225 S combat missions and 15 full Gates → 225 S missions and 15 more full Gates → Spire 12 examination → Spire 15 examination → Spire 16 examination | Bearer of the Four Seals |
| V | The First Mythic Survey | 450 S combat missions and 30 full Gates → 450 S missions and 30 more full Gates → Spire 17 examination → Spire 18 examination → Spire 20 examination | Witness of the Mythic Age |

Every era requires world-era availability and the preceding personal campaign's completed version-two receipt (I has no predecessor). IV also requires level 70 and a known chosen Legacy at Stage IV (Proven) or higher. V requires level 100 and that Legacy at Stage V (summit); owning mythic rarity is not required. Existing permanent Legacy stages satisfy admission. World V still needs its separate community unlock. Existing missions, Gate access, wars, bosses, Legacy eligibility and all Legacy trial stages keep their admission rules. Titles add identity rather than combat power or currency. Choosing a perspective commits that choice; it cannot bypass workload or examinations.

Only victories from the four built-in, server-resolved C/B/A/S combat missions count for all campaigns; higher ranks satisfy a lower-rank requirement. Fetch jobs, Hunter Guild contracts, duels, custom mission slots and aggregate Legacy counters do not count. IV/V require the S-rank crisis mission. Mission proof uses the victory timestamp, so an old win claimed after a stage opens is still old evidence. Stage transitions consume the final qualifying event only in that stage. Missions won before the next stage opens cannot be banked for it.

The mission board's built-in A-Rank Hunt counts; Hunter Guild hunts are a separate activity. Rank access is C at level 15, B at 30, A at 50 and S at 70. Thus I–III reach level 30, 50 and 70 requirements respectively. Combat mission rewards do not supply leveling XP; players still need the game's stat-growth activities to reach those levels. Campaign acceptance does not replace normal mission or Tower eligibility.

A full Gate means a five-floor run started during the current stage, with the final boss resolved, extraction from floor five, and a committed settlement. Three-floor variants, early extractions, abandonment and replay do not count. Workload stages containing missions and Gates require both objectives before advancing.

The normal Gate allowance is two starts per UTC day, with one extra start from the extra-dive attunement. Failed dives consume attempts, and normal entries require a Gate key. These costs remain part of expedition progression. Story examinations use sequentially unlocked floors at level 30 or above. Replaying an already cleared Story floor is free and consumes no daily entry slot; uncleared floors retain three free entries followed by a 1,500 Ryo toll. An examination is not itself a mandatory multi-day wait.

I–III examinations reuse the actual Story Tower encounters. Start a fresh run after the examination stage opens; the same squad victory must have no fighter knockouts, clear within the floor's scoring par, and include a disrupted pylon. Floor 7 and 9 also require an avoided signature strike; floor 10 requires baiting a charge into a pillar. Existing par is 14 rounds on floor 5, 16 on floors 7 and 9, and 18 on floor 10. Old honors and personal bests cannot be combined across runs. These are squad feats, not proof that each member individually disrupted the pylon. Spire, embedded encounters and clan bosses cannot substitute for the named Story floors. Tower level and floor-unlock eligibility remain enforced by the Tower system.

The workload totals are 60, 150 and 300 qualified victories, with Gates and examinations in addition. At an assumed five qualifying victories per active day, that is 12, 30 and 60 active days before additional examination attempts. These are arithmetic scenarios, not measured completion times or enforced calendar waits. The existing shared mission cap is 20 claims per day; skilled, highly active players can progress faster. Tower challenge depends on the squad's build and coordination. This change does not implement normalized-power examinations or retune the Story bosses.

Combined I–III require 510 qualified claims, so at least 26 daily mission allowances under ordinary player limits, even with all 20 spent on qualified wins. Spending five slots on daily growth fetch jobs leaves 15 qualified claims and raises that arithmetic bound to 34 allowances. This is not a promise of 26 or 34 full elapsed days: UTC boundaries, losses, leveling, keys and examination attempts affect actual completion. The ordinary authored mission enemies have fixed rank-based levels; late builds can outgrow them. Volume establishes sustained participation, not individual mastery.

The era screen states the reward before unlocking, links to activities that contribute to the world effort, identifies every unmet requirement without an averaged “nearness” claim, and refreshes visible era/personal progress every 30 seconds. Successful chapter actions also trigger an immediate authoritative refresh, so sealing a predecessor reveals the next campaign without waiting for polling. World status labels explicitly say WORLD UNLOCKED, WORLD IN PROGRESS or WORLD SEALED; personal completion is shown separately. Waived community goals render as satisfied. Completed chapters preserve the route's conclusion and provide a link to wear the title in Profile. Historical records for all five eras offer only their original perspective, matching the server's immutable-route rule.

IV/V examinations use public Endless Spire tiers. Each requires a fresh victory within the sealed encounter's scoring par, no squad knockout, and pylon disruption. Tier 15 also requires charge baiting into a pillar; 12/16/17/18/20 require signature avoidance. Par is 18/20/16/18/18/20 rounds respectively, distinct from the blessing-extended timeout. Public Spire launches require four live players in a bound ready room and sequential tier access for every member. Story recruits, borrowed AI, short-party practice, same-number Story floors and embedded encounters cannot substitute. Campaign evidence requires four distinct live player owners. Each eligible owner progresses only their own active stage. These remain squad accomplishments and can include strong teammates; they are not individually attributable normalized trials.

The rebased mission/Gate workloads rise from III's 300/20 to IV's 450/30 and V's 900/60. At five qualified wins per active day the IV/V mission components require 90/180 active days; at ten they require 45/90. With the ordinary 20-claim cap, IV needs at least 23 daily allowances and V at least 45. Two-stage final-slot waste can increase those bounds. All five require 1,860 qualified mission victories, at least 93 allowances before stage boundaries and other claims. Normal two-start Gate limits imply at least 15/30 allowances for IV/V's successful full descents (10/20 with extra-dive attunement). Mission and Gate work in a stage can run concurrently. These are lower-bound arithmetic scenarios, not promised wall-clock durations or human measurements.

The former IV hunts/boss-damage and V discovery/early-extraction alternatives no longer award personal campaign completion. Those activities continue to contribute to their existing Legacy and world systems. Master/Grandmaster titles stay cosmetic, with version-two campaign completion providing the new achievement authority; old title ownership alone is never mastery admission.

## Authority and recovery

- `shared/era-chapters.ts` owns chapter prose, perspectives, stages, objectives, sequence, IV/V admission floors and cosmetic titles. `api/_era-campaign.ts` validates version-two authority and records qualifying source receipts. Mission payout, Gate settlement and Tower record settlement call it inside their authoritative player-save transaction. Every personal campaign is independent of aggregate Legacy activity counters; IV/V admission reads their server-owned chosen Legacy stage.
- Authenticated `GET /api/eras/journey?playerName=…` returns only the owner's chapter progress. `POST` accepts `start` or `complete` with a known era and route. Every action checks era availability; clients cannot supply counters, baselines, completion timestamps, or reward ownership.
- Starting any campaign opens stage one with zero counters and a stage timestamp. Every stage must finish in order; its transition records a conclusion receipt and resets current counters. Unique run receipts remain for the whole campaign, bounded by its objective total. Replaying a start preserves that campaign; a different perspective cannot overwrite it. Completing requires every stage, examination and admission condition.
- Historical version-one records for all five eras never satisfy a new campaign or successor admission. Accepting the same perspective after meeting admission migrates to version two with fresh proof, preserving any old completion separately as `legacyCompletedAt` and retaining earned cosmetic titles. The IV `boss` and V `gate` route identities remain supported as narrative perspectives. Generic image-preserving save merges replace the complete `eraJourneys` subtree so spent counters and an old `completedAt` cannot reappear during transition or migration.
- `character.eraJourneys` is a bounded map of five server-owned records. Save ownership rules preserve stored records on ordinary saves, including first-save anti-forgery and flag-off protection, and strip them from combat projections. Malformed stored records fail closed.
- Completion and the title vault write occur in one versioned player-save transaction. Completion retries preserve the receipt timestamp and do not duplicate the reward. The client validates the returned player, route, title and save version, and adopts it through the host's version fence.
- Era unlock announcements use a stable receipt for feed and village-chat delivery. Existing unfinished feed records are adopted before retry. Hall and finisher title delivery must be confirmed before `era:effects-done` is written. A later daily pass retries unfinished effects. The title save uses a fail-closed lock.
- World-state reads validate the override map and propagate unavailable or damaged authority. An absent row alone uses authored launch defaults. Legacy acceptance stamps the highest actually unlocked world era, independently of personal campaign completion; a failed state read leaves acceptance retryable instead of inventing a birth era. Unlock, finisher title, effects marker and admin status/milestone writes require confirmed storage success. An unfinished title delivery repairs both ownership lists without duplicating the title.
- World discoveries use durable per-player settlement fields, so two players resolving the same road wanderer contribute independently and retrying one player's settlement contributes once. Discoveries and war-battle sources do not use the pending ledger capped at 256, because their handlers have no acknowledgement saga. Old raw and ledger totals remain readable alongside the new receipt fields. No historic ownership or missing credit is reconstructed from anonymous totals.
- Wanderer gift/service/quest/ambush handlers require both the Legacy receipt and the world contribution to finish. Failed delivery returns a retryable response; recovered gift claims preserve the already paid reward. Legacy activity baseline and receipt writes also require confirmation. Journey GET no longer reads those sidecar counters for any personal campaign, so a counter outage cannot hide valid campaign progress.
- Gate/Tower proof rejects nonfinite, nonpositive or future start timestamps relative to settlement, and campaign completion cannot predate its final stage. These checks strengthen damaged-record handling; qualifying requirements are unchanged.

Existing unlocks already marked complete by the old implementation are not reopened automatically; reconstructing lost historic credit needs a separate evidence-backed repair. The fixed delivery rules apply to future unlocks and unfinished transactions. Existing Era V requirements and historical global counters were preserved. This change adds personal participation through chapters; it does not retroactively attribute anonymous global contributions or introduce population-based retuning.

## Validation

Automated coverage checks rank qualification, every sequential campaign stage, short/old/abandoned Gate rejection, same-run examination conditions, historical migration, prior-campaign admission, immutable perspectives, atomic reward-write failure and replay. Handler integration tests exercise ordinary and reserved/recovered combat payouts, real Gate settlements and Tower record settlements. Save tests cover spent-counter clearing and server ownership; client tests reject stale campaign versions and mismatched stage receipts. Earlier announcement/title recovery coverage remains applicable.

Browser QA uses the real chapter component and Hall with controlled API replies at desktop and mobile sizes, checks perspective → both workload stages → examination → conclusion → profile navigation, blocked II and locked V behavior, historical migration, immediate next-campaign admission, overflow and chapter accessibility, and captures perspective/examination/completion screenshots. Server endpoints and settlement handlers are separately tested with isolated memory KV. The Tower endpoint regression injects a campaign save failure, verifies recovery and nonduplicate payout, seals the title, and reads the newly admitted Era II through the actual journey handler. Authored-floor mechanics checks cover usable pylons, signature avoidance and charge-pillar opportunities on floors 5, 7, 9 and 10; they isolate mechanics and do not establish complete human win rates.
