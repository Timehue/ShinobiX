# Era campaign integration review

Historical snapshot: the IV/V short-objective and missing-predecessor finding below was subsequently resolved by the owner-authorized [Master/Grandmaster implementation](master-campaign-implementation.md). Its current evidence supersedes this review's IV/V personal-rule snapshot. The world-contribution/authority fixes and I–III connections remain applicable; current campaign rules live in [World Era Chapters](../../era-chapters.md).

Date: October 1, 2026 (America/Chicago).
Status: completed working-tree audit and integration corrections. No deployment or production save changes.
Question: Are personal eras connected to actual activities, saves, rewards and UI, and does the five-era progression make sense?
Authority: [Current campaign rules](../../era-chapters.md) describe the implemented I–III campaigns. [IV/V research](endgame-research.md) remains a proposal. Repository code proves current behavior; local fixtures prove the tested paths. No live environment, player telemetry or human difficulty cohort was inspected.

## Verdict

I–III are integrated sequential campaigns with authoritative fresh proof, reachable activity prerequisites and permanent cosmetic rewards. The audit corrected Hall refresh, historical perspective selection and misleading activity/Weekly Boss instructions. The full five-era ladder still fails the intended difficulty curve: IV/V retain their small independent chapters. The first three also need content variety and measured difficulty before their workload can be called engaging or personal mastery.

The author's second verification request expanded the check to world counters, override authority, admin edits and Legacy birth stamps. That pass found real correctness defects beyond the first review: discoveries could stall at 256, different players' shared-wanderer contributions could collide, and failed state reads could invent launch defaults. These are now fixed and covered by regressions below.

## Progression and pacing logic

Rank admission is C15/B30/A50/S70. I reaches B and the level-30 Tower; II reaches A50 and Story 7; III reaches S70 and Story 9/10. The [Story eligibility service](../../../api/towers/_story-eligibility.ts) requires each human's sequential floor access or an authoritative prior clear. There is no circular dependency requiring campaign completion to unlock these activities. Combat claims do not award leveling XP; normal stat-growth activities still supply character progression.

The normal 20-claim daily cap implies at least 26 daily allowances for 510 qualifying wins across I–III; five daily fetch jobs reduce available qualifying slots to 15 and imply 34 allowances. At five qualified wins per active day, the separate campaign workloads take 12/30/60 active days. These are arithmetic assumptions, not observed duration or full wall-clock-day guarantees. Gate keys, two daily starts (three with attunement), losses, leveling and examinations add dependencies. Gates and missions within a workload stage can progress concurrently. [Current rules](../../era-chapters.md) retain the detailed requirements and costs.

Rewards currently establish narrative identity and cosmetic prestige. Personal era completion does not unlock a new combat power, mission tier, Gate access or world-era status. Old title holders keep the same cosmetic title and must earn a separate v2 completion record. This is coherent with current campaign rules, but substantial era-specific gameplay payoffs remain future design work.

World Era V still requires 5,000 missions, 1,500 validated duel wins, 400 resolved wanderer encounters, 150 qualifying Gate extractions and 25 Legacy awakenings, plus a first mythic-rarity awakening. That trigger is the Legacy stage 1→2 awakening, not the stage 4→5 Mythic trial. It can precede the community totals; the first finisher remains credited when the later totals unlock the world. The highest unlocked world era supplies `eraBorn`; completing a personal campaign does not change that stamp or the world state. Existing historical totals remain preserved. Any repair of old anonymous receipts or missing per-player credit requires separate evidence; this pass does not reconstruct them.
