# Pet Arena integration and balance review

Question: do the two-player, 12-species PvP paths use one combat authority, retain
player agency, communicate their actual rules, and resist the original signature
and button-spam problems?

Expected behavior comes from [the prototype specification](../../pet-tactics-prototype.md)
and [the balance plan](../../pet-battle-balance-plan-2026-10-10.md). Numeric runtime
owners are `shared/pet-tactics-roster.ts` and `api/_pet-tactics/engine.ts`. This is
a local implementation review; production settings and human playtests were not
inspected. No numeric tuning was promoted from these simulations.

## Balance experiment

The reproducible [matchup report](matchup-report.json) records source hashes and
fixture seed 581309. Its harness runs the real resolver, using public-state
heuristics. It randomizes squads/leads, both presets, custom legal move selections,
all three items and six legal allocation extremes. The random custom-build
probability is 40%; it does not imply 40% optimal builds.

The run includes 2,400 paired-seat self-play matches, 1,440 paired spam comparisons,
240 exact-squad mirrors and 8,448 controlled lead substitutions. In the latter,
teammates, opposition, allocation, item and seed remain fixed while the lead
species/kit changes. Each species has 352 such fixtures. These controls improve
coverage but still evaluate one bounded policy and two preset kits.

Observed results across **12,528 matches**:

- Median length: 15 rounds; 90th percentile: 20. Every match terminated by
  knockout, including 315 simultaneous knockout draws; none hit the 30-round cap.
- Tactical score: 60.97% against highest-damage choices and 64.03% against
  signature-first choices. Guard-only and passive sustain policies lost all
  tested comparisons. These deliberately limited policies are diagnostic probes.
- Seat A scored 49.46% in paired self-play and 48.96% in exact mirrors. No material
  seat advantage appeared at the harness's predeclared 40–60% mirror gate.
- Conditional squad scores by species ranged from 45.33% to 57.01%. These include
  shared teammates and are not isolated species win rates.
- Controlled substitutions placed Storm Hawk at -9.09 and Gale Chick at -7.32
  percentage points of team score relative to sampled alternatives. Abyssal
  Leviathan and Granite Tortoise were strongest at +7.53 and +7.10 points.
  This is a tuning watch list, not evidence for an immediate stat nerf/buff.

Across **24,192 signature probes**, including extreme allocations, Rally, Expose,
Sky and stacked setup, there were no unprepared full-health knockouts, incidental
splash hits or cap violations. Maximum observed damage was 59.89% unprepared,
74.91% prepared and 37.50% with first-use Guard, including stacked preparation.
The probes also verify the stamina cost. The original 960-match policy audit
reproduced identically; charge/hold, cleanse, switch targeting and control-immunity
regressions remain authoritative for their distinct rules.

Decision: retain the current experimental numeric configuration. The automated
evidence supports substantially safer burst, useful defensive/support choices and
an advantage for resource/target decisions over the tested spam policies. It does
not establish a final competitive meta. The harness does not optimize move pools,
team synergy, every utility interaction or human reads. Structured human sessions
and held-out optimized builds should revisit the four watch-list species before
release. Full owned-roster and NPC expansion remain separate plan gates.

## Validation

The final selected integration, authority, navigation and presentation run passes
256 tests. Reproduce the expanded simulation with:

```powershell
node --import tsx scripts/pet-tactics-matchup-audit.mts
```

The current [browser report](ranked-browser-report.json) and the audit directory's
[acceptance notes](README.md) own full-match and build evidence. The final browser
run completed a 14-round ranked match with one Elo receipt per player, private
drafts, reconnect, resource updates, search cancellation and return-to-builder
checks. Client/server builds, packaging and the unchanged code-size gates pass.
No deployment,
production KV drill or new human playtest is claimed.
