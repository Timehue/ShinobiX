# Pet Home art manifest

The original asset suite was generated through `shinobij.client/scripts/gen-asset.mjs`; the later hatch-sanctum quality pass used the built-in image-generation workflow with the selected Home and barn scenes as explicit style references. Every production selection was visually reviewed, encoded as WebP, and integrated from `shinobij.client/src/assets`.

## Selected scenes

- `pet-home/home-hero.webp` and `facilities/home.webp` — selected Home candidate B, 1536×1024 generation, medium quality. Prompt: “Wide cinematic premium pet sanctuary interior courtyard in an original shinobi fantasy village at dusk, cedar galleries and arched tiled roofs surrounding a peaceful garden, silk banners bearing abstract paw crests, lantern trails, shallow reflective pond, luxurious nesting shelters, elegant breeding barn at rear center with five subtle elemental lantern colors, open foreground and left side for readable game interface, painterly AAA RPG environment, no characters, no animals, no words.”
