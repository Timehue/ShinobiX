# Shinobi Journey — Frostfang: The Choice to Return

Target: 38–42 second landing-page cinematic teaser. No gameplay footage. Rill is the player lead.

## Dramatic question

If a village can guarantee that nobody is abandoned, is it allowed to take away everyone's ability to leave?

## Trailer promise

Frostfang does not have a generic evil ruler or a monster problem. It has a system that saves lives and quietly removes consent. Rill arrives as the one person the system cannot classify, carries proof that people can choose to return, and must decide what replaces compulsory safety.

## Story arc

### 1. Promise — 0:00–0:09

- Bell in a whiteout; lanterns connect the village.
- Rill places his wrist on the intake plate. It answers with the reflection of someone long dead.
- Kael carries a frostbitten shepherd home through lethal weather.
- Copy: `NO ONE IS LEFT BEHIND.`

The trailer first proves why Frostfang believes in the Count. Kael must look like a leader worth following, not a stock villain.

### 2. Price — 0:09–0:20

- The litany arrives one phrase at a time: `CHECKED. COUNTED. KEPT. WARM.`
- Rill and Yura enter the White Silence: forty-three citizens preserved alive because they doubted, refused, or missed a check.
- Copy: `FROSTFANG SAVES EVERYONE.` then `EVEN FROM THE PART THAT WANTED TO LEAVE.`

This is the reversal. The same mechanism that saves the shepherd freezes the dissenter.

### 3. Choice — 0:20–0:30

- Yura removes her own oath mark. The broken script dissolves between her and Rill.
- Her canonical idea appears in compressed form: `IF THEY COME FOR ME NOW, THEY CHOSE TO.`
- Rill carries Dren Coldewe's relay lantern down to the vault. Its flame is evidence: rescuers found a man in a whiteout without a mark or the machine.
- Kael stands at the meter as its needle hits zero.

The lantern is the story object connecting freedom to survival. Rill is not merely opposing Kael; he brings another answer.

### 4. Collision — 0:30–0:36

- Kael: `SHOW ME WHAT HOLDS WHEN NOTHING IS HOLDING IT.`
- Kael's ice wave crosses the vault. Rill wolf-steps around it while protecting the relay flame.
- The fight is brief because the audience now understands what both men are fighting for.

### 5. Player promise — 0:36–0:42

- Three decisions strike in rhythm against close details of the zero meter and Rill's hand:
  - `BREAK EVERY MARK.`
  - `BIND THE VAULT.`
  - `TAKE THE VALVE.`
- Main title: `SHINOBI JOURNEY`
- Subtitle: `THE OATH MUST BREAK`
- CTA: `BEGIN YOUR JOURNEY`
- Disclosure: `CINEMATIC TRAILER — NOT ACTUAL GAMEPLAY`

Do not resolve Rill's choice. The landing-page promise is that the player owns it.

## Copy discipline

- Use no generic phrases such as destiny, chosen one, ancient evil or save the world.
- Keep every card to one short sentence or fragment.
- Let the rescue image sell Kael's argument; do not explain it twice.
- Let the White Silence sell the horror; do not add graphic violence.
- Keep Rill silent. His interiority is expressed through Yura's decision, the lantern he protects and the final player choice.
