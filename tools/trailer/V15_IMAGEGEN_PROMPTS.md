- Keyframes were created with the built-in `image_gen` tool in edit mode.
- Every local edit target was inspected before generation.
- Selected outputs were copied into `tmp/trailer/cinematic-v15/` and consumed from the workspace.
- Motion was generated locally with FramePack, then audited with contact sheets. Failed variants were rejected.
