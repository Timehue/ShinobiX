# Level 50-100 Endgame Audit

Date: July 7, 2026

## Verdict

The level 50-100 game has many endgame hooks, but only a subset should be promoted during first beta. Jonin+ players can test advanced PvE, pets, ranked systems, professions, and selected village loops; high-impact world-state and shared-reward systems should stay gated or staffed.

## Current Endgame Anchors

| Area | Current state |
| --- | --- |
| Rank bands | Jonin at 50, Special Jonin at 80. |
| Mission bands | A-tier at 50, S-tier at 70. |
| Stat caps | Jonin cap applies until Special Jonin; Special Jonin reaches global stat cap. |
| Level cap | 100. XP is clamped at max level. |
| Hollow Gate | Late-game, dense, no-retreat pressure; already marked desktop-first beta. |
| Battle Towers/Endless Tower | Monitored beta systems, not first-session promises. |
| Weekly Boss | Client damage is gated unless explicitly enabled. |
| Village/Sector War | High-impact social/economy systems, gate or soft-launch with staff. |
| Bloodline Maker/player AI image generation | Created content requires moderation; player AI image generation defaults disabled. |

## What Is Safe To Promote In First Beta

- A-tier and S-tier missions as progression goals.
- Battle Towers as monitored advanced PvE after mobile smoke.
- Endless Tower as a repeatable goal after reward pacing checks.
- Pet Arena and Pet Ladder as monitored side progression.
- Professions and profession mastery as long-term identity.
- Hall of Legends/reputation as aspirational, not required.

## What Should Stay Gated Or Staffed

- Weekly Boss contribution rewards until server-authoritative settlement is enabled.
- Broad Village/Sector War seasons until admin coverage and reward receipts are reviewed.
- Hollow Gate mobile promotion until dense controls pass mobile smoke.
- Bloodline Maker promotion until moderation and rollback process is staffed.
- Player AI image generation unless spend and moderation are explicitly approved.

## Endgame Balance Risks

| Risk | Why it matters | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared reward inflation | War crates, boss rewards, and rare currencies can affect the whole economy | Keep gated/staffed and inspect receipts. |
| Low-population dominance | A small group can own PvP, villages, or leaderboards | Avoid permanent seasons until population is stable. |
| Dense mobile UI | Endgame combat and Hollow Gate have many controls/states | Treat as desktop-first until screenshots pass. |
| Content creator abuse | Player-created content can become balance, moderation, or spend risk | Keep reviewed/admin-only for first beta. |
| Progression cliff after 70 | High rewards exist, but content cadence must feel intentional | Use weekly goals and patch notes, not broad XP buffs. |

## Recommendation

Do not advertise level 50-100 as fully balanced. First beta should frame it as monitored advanced content and collect:

1. Completion rates for A/S missions and towers.
2. PvP win-rate outliers by level band.
3. Ryo and rare currency balances for level 50+ players.
4. Endgame stuck-state reports.
5. Manual moderation load for any created content tested.
