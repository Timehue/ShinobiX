# Clan mission icon art

These eight transparent source images were generated as a coordinated first-pass icon set for the Clan Hall mission cards. They follow the game's dark shinobi fantasy: worn forged materials, indigo bindings, aged gold, restrained cinnabar accents, and warm upper-left lighting. The images were previewed together against the Clan Hall's dark blue panel color before export to 256 px WebP files in `public/ui/clan-missions/`.

The attached collar and achievement badge were used as style references, not copied as subjects.

## Generation prompt

Shared prompt used for each image:

> Create one square video-game inventory icon as a standalone image. Use the attached references only for art direction: richly material-rendered fantasy prop art from a Japanese shinobi world, tactile metal/leather/paper, controlled hand-painted realism, charcoal and deep indigo shadows, restrained aged gold and cinnabar accents, directional warm rim light from upper-left. Center one unmistakable focal object, fill about 78 percent of frame, make the silhouette and main read clear at 64 px, moderate detail, transparent background. No UI frame, no background scene, no text, no lettering, no watermark, no extra objects, no photorealistic product-photo look.

Subject prompts, in source-file order:

- `battle`: a pair of crossed kunai with wrapped handles and forged steel blades, a few restrained ember sparks attached to the metal only.
- `mission`: one tightly rolled parchment mission scroll bound with dark red cord and a small stamped wax seal; clear readable silhouette.
- `guard`: one compact lacquered forearm guard shield with a subtle leaf crest engraved in aged gold, dark blue-black lacquer and visible edge wear.
- `territory`: one folded hand-drawn shinobi region map with a single small red banner pin planted in a marked district, no writing or labels.
- `anbu`: one dark ivory fox-anbu half mask with understated carved lines, a small indigo cord tie and a single muted crimson accent; mysterious but not horror.
- `donation`: a short stack of aged gold ryō coins, one upright coin embossed with a simple original leaf-and-wave crest, no currency letters or numbers.
- `training`: one scarred wooden makiwara practice post wrapped with worn indigo hand wraps, a couple of embedded training kunai; compact and clear.
- `raid`: one cracked iron oni boss mask with one broken horn and subtle red light in the fracture, dark steel and ash; heroic game loot art, not gore.
