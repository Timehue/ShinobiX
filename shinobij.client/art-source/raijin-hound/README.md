# Raijin Hound production source

The current game model comes from the user-supplied three-view turnaround and three-quarter character art in this directory. One multi-view Hunyuan3D Pro v3.1 request was submitted on 2026-09-23 (`01a0cfc7-b6c9-7e50-8579-f88687fad32c`), within the approved $0.75 USD ceiling. `pro-generation-request.json` and `pro-generation-job.json` record the request and downloaded result. The quoted request price was $0.675; the provider's final charge was not independently verified.

`pro-generation-candidate.glb` is the untouched 495,976-triangle PBR sculpt (SHA-256 `5992e4e27f4dd8af10d05f9b92a7d9074af8032fb8e26bced8f5c98ef5fa4b1c`). `raijin-pro-rig-candidate-v3.blend` is the editable, reduced sculpt with the existing 21-bone quadruped rig and corrected head weights (SHA-256 `8a52e4870371fa31f40de692739640a5bde1a35a6096a0e205f31cb5230502a5`). The reduction, weight transfer, and compression steps are in `scripts/prepare-raijin-pro-mesh.py`, `scripts/rig-raijin-pro-candidate.py`, and `scripts/optimize-raijin-pro-candidate.mjs`.

The shipped base and showcase GLBs are `public/pet-models/starter-lightning-l.glb` and `public/pet-models/showdown-v2/starter-lightning-l.glb`. The showcase has 13 authored animation clips: `idle`, `idle_2`, `walk`, `gallop`, `gallop_jump`, `attack`, `idle_hitreact1`, `death`, `entrance`, `cast`, `guard`, `rest`, and `victory`. `raijin-hound-showdown-animated.blend` is the editable version of that battle asset, with the skinned sculpt, 21-bone rig, packed textures, and all 13 actions. Recreate it by decoding the showcase GLB with `scripts/decode-raijin-animation-source.mjs`, then importing the result with `scripts/create-raijin-animated-blend.py` in Blender. Keep `raijin-pro-rig-candidate-v3.blend` as the earlier sculpt and rig work file.

The live 2D pose files in `public/pet-poses/starter-lightning-l-*.webp` are rendered from the current animated `.blend` with `scripts/render-raijin-pose-sprites.py` and published with `scripts/finalize-raijin-pose-sprites.mjs`. The evolution portrait in `public/pet-evos/starter-lightning-l.webp` comes from the supplied three-quarter character art. `raijin-pose-review-v2.png` shows the complete pose set. `src/lib/pet-art-revision.ts` invalidates cached copies, including the portrait paths saved on existing pets.

The active distant assets are `public/pet-models/warfront-lod/showdown-v2/starter-lightning-l.glb` and `public/pet-models/warfront-impostors/showdown-v2/starter-lightning-l.webp`; their manifest entries resolve from the active showcase GLB. The older procedural GLBs, earlier rig `.blend` versions, previous-live backups, and obsolete comparison captures have been removed. Retain the untouched high-poly source and the v3 rig file for further sculpt and skinning work; neither is loaded by the game.

The current sculpt faces local +Z in the combat renderer. The animation artist pass adds articulated lower legs, neck, chest, and tail to the eight core clips, plus distinct entrance, cast, guard, rest, and victory performances. Defeat now ends in a grounded side collapse with the tail within the fallen silhouette; the game player holds that settle pose through the result screen. The distant battle LOD has 17,902 triangles, includes the same 13 clips, and meets the 0.975 silhouette score (minimum measured 0.9754). Its three PBR maps are reduced from 2048px to 1024px for distant use, saving about 1.2 MB in the release artifact; its derived impostor atlas was regenerated. `compare-oni-raijin-final.png` is a same-lighting, four-angle browser capture of the shipped model and the existing Oni Hound. `raijin-animation-art-pass-v1.png` and `raijin-defeat-art-pass-v1.png` record the live battle-preview review of this pass.

The supplied illustration is a style target. This generated sculpt is a substantial improvement over the prior procedural mesh, but the eyes, asymmetric paw pose, and fur edges still need a final sculpt and texture pass before calling it AAA quality. Automated sampling of 155 poses still reports 24 skin deformation warnings around the head, shoulder, paws, and tail in some extremes. A broad weight-remap candidate detached paw armor in live review and was rejected; this pass instead limits the leg arcs to the current skin's safe range. The unchanged 495,976-triangle source is retained for further work.
