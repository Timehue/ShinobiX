# Celestial Lion source

`Celestial Lion` is the final name for the user supplied white winged lion. The historical source directory and scripts retain their Dawnmane working name; the runtime identity is `mythic-15`. The original file is `reference.png`. The user later supplied `reference-views-user.png`, showing the intended single tail from front, side, and back. The 1024px transparent WebP portrait is published at `public/pet-portraits/breeding-mythics/mythic-15.webp`.

The side and back reference images were made with the built-in ImageGen edit tool from the user image, then cut from `turnaround.png` into `views/left.png`, `views/back.png`, and `views/right.png`. The exact generation prompt was:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: orthographic 3D modeling turnaround reference for a game creature. Edit/reference target: the supplied white winged lion. Produce ONE clean wide image divided into THREE equal panels with the SAME creature in left profile, straight rear, and right profile. Preserve exact identity: large white lion with long cream mane and tail tuft, four lion paws and feline face, TWO huge feathered white wings with fine gold feather edges attached at the shoulder blades, TWO curved segmented gold horns, sunburst gold mark on forehead, amber eyes, ornate thin gold solar lines and stars across shoulders and flank. Show the full body from wing tip to paw with no cropping; all views use consistent anatomy, wing size, markings and color. Neutral standing pose, wings extended enough to reveal their feather surfaces and attachment. Even studio lighting, flat light gray background, no floor shadows, no labels, no text, no extra creatures, no equipment. Make this useful as a precise multi-view input to image-to-3D.

`fal-request.json` records the original four view Hunyuan 3D Pro request (`01a0d627-3f27-7ee2-957c-2ecf97fc9176`). Its mesh had two physical tails and was rejected after the user's rear view check. `fal-replacement-request.json` records the accepted replacement (`01a0d657-11bd-7231-a40b-cb50f400b637`): the user's newer clean front panel plus the left and right profiles, with no back input. The supplied back panel was retained for anatomy review; leaving it out of generation avoided interpreting the same curled tail as a second centered one. The two guarded submissions total an estimated $1.35, within the user's $2 ceiling. Each $0.675 estimate comes from the [fal model listing](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/hunyuan-3d/v3.1/pro/image-to-3d): $0.375 base, $0.15 PBR, and $0.15 multi-view. `fal-attempt.json`, `fal-job.json`, `fal-replacement-attempt.json`, and `fal-replacement-job.json` preserve the request, input hashes, and download receipts. The actual billed amount requires the provider account receipt.

The shipped model is `public/pet-models/roster/mythic-15.glb`. Both raw fal downloads are retained locally and excluded from Git. `scripts/prepare-dawnmane-mesh.mjs 0.08 0.03 --replacement` reproduces `prepared-replacement-source.glb`, a 2.5 MB reduced source with 2,048px WebP textures. `scripts/rig-breeding-mythics.mjs mythic-15` builds the 27-bone mesh, and `scripts/author-all-pet-animations.mjs mythic-15` adds the final 13-clip bank: `idle`, `idle_2`, `walk`, `gallop`, `gallop_jump`, `attack`, `idle_hitreact1`, `death`, `entrance`, `cast`, `guard`, `rest`, and `victory`. This is the same complete clip contract as the other production pets. The wing roots and midsections receive tracks in every clip. The Wind art direction adds a turquoise and ivory rotating current, mint and gold move tints, and a spiral gale burst on the flagship impact.

## Game kit

This is a wild-capturable and breedable Wind Mythic defender/tank. Its five seed techniques are Zephyr Aegis (barrier), Galeclaw Pounce (damage), Tempest Roar (damage), Cloudborne Mending (heal), and Skywing Leap (movement). Balance adds Heaven's Vortex (confuse), the flagship Celestial Tempest: Lion's Descent (crush), and Gale Challenge (taunt).

The replacement mesh has 45,222 vertices and 39,718 triangles. Four-angle raw, reduced, idle, gallop, cast, victory, and LOD reviews show one tail and no second tuft. The Warfront LOD has 16,948 triangles with a minimum three-view silhouette overlap of 0.9760, and the 16-frame impostor atlas is `public/pet-models/warfront-impostors/roster/mythic-15.webp`. The dense feather geometry requires the documented Dawnmane-specific LOD budget in `scripts/generate-warfront-pet-lods.mjs`.

For release review, `replacement-prepared-review.png` shows the approved one-tail geometry, and `celestial-lion-colosseum-idle.png` and `celestial-lion-colosseum-battle.png` show the pet in the battle renderer. The other intermediate renders and the large raw fal downloads remain local authoring evidence.

## Production acceptance

- One complete lion with four legs, two horns, two shoulder-attached feathered wings, mane, and exactly one tail with one tuft; no second body or detached proxy.
- One textured mesh primitive with normals, UVs, embedded base-color atlas, and 8,000–60,000 vertices, matching the game's structural certification limits. Keep the production color atlas at 2,048px or smaller; LOD textures are capped at 1,024px.
- A 27-bone quadruped-plus-wings skin with normalized weights and all 13 clips above. The lion faces the combat renderer's +Z convention after visual review.
- Preserve both user images as provenance. The original side and rear references came from ImageGen; the final replacement used the user's new front panel and those generated left/right profiles. The fal output license must be checked against the connected account terms before external redistribution.
