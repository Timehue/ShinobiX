# Kage Tactics fortress backdrop

Generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool and integrated as a
distant CSS backdrop. The playable board is real Three.js geometry; this image
is never used as a floor texture.

Final prompt:

- Primary request: a moonlit shinobi mountain fortress courtyard surrounding a foreground battle platform, with layered tiled rooftops, misty cedar valley, distant pagodas and cliff waterfalls

- Composition: ultra-wide 16:9 horizon-level three-quarter backdrop; quiet dark negative space through the lower center where a real foreground 3D platform will sit; architecture frames the left and right edges; strong depth layers; no playable floor or board painted into the image
- Lighting: moonlit indigo sky, warm paper-lantern accents, restrained cyan clan light on far left and ember-red clan light on far right, volumetric mist
